## 1. Output template

For each planned frame, specify:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Role | Establish, reveal, action, detail, transition, or closure |
| Frame | Orientation, scale, camera height, and subject placement |
| Spatial idea | Line, threshold, reflection, symmetry, depth, shadow, or negative space |
| Action | One natural action or trace |
| Light | Direction, softness, color, and time window |
| Lens | Practical equivalent focal range |
| Exposure intent | Freeze, controlled blur, shallow focus, or spatial clarity |
| Continuity | Color, prop, gesture, or location link to adjacent frames |

## 2. Slow-travel photo essay

Build 10–12 frames:

1. **Arrival:** wide geography or architecture with a small sign of human scale.
2. **Threshold:** door, ferry rail, curtain, alley, or window that implies entry.
3. **Local rhythm:** an ordinary activity observed without spectacle.
4. **Material detail:** stone, tile, linen, vegetation, salt, or worn paint.
5. **Quiet portrait:** a person absorbed in an action, preferably off-camera.
6. **Table or object:** one believable arrangement that shows time passing.
7. **Movement:** wind, water, walking, transport, or fabric.
8. **Graphic light:** a frame organized by sun and shadow.
9. **Intimate fragment:** hand, shoulder, reflection, footprints, or open book.
10. **Breathing frame:** generous negative space.
11. **Light transition:** blue hour, fading sun, or interior practical light.
12. **Departure:** an unresolved, quiet closing image rather than a grand finale.

Keep at least three image scales and two orientations. Repeat one subtle motif for cohesion, not the same composition.

## 3. Boutique hotel and architecture

Build 8–10 frames:

- exterior in geographic context;
- entry sequence or framed approach;
- signature room showing circulation and proportion;
- window-to-view relationship;
- architectural line or shadow study;
- material close-up;
- lived-in bed, table, bath, or balcony;
- small human gesture for scale;
- transition between indoor and outdoor;
- dusk or night atmosphere.

Correct verticals where appropriate, but keep the space human rather than diagrammatic. Avoid sterile perfection: introduce one plausible sign of use. For commercial work, photograph required amenities clearly in separate coverage rather than forcing every sales point into the editorial frames.

## 4. Lifestyle portrait or couple

Build 7–9 frames around a shared activity:

- environmental wide frame with subjects small;
- walking or crossing the space;
- one quiet profile or three-quarter portrait;
- hands interacting with a local object;
- layered frame through a doorway or curtain;
- moving garment, hair, water, or shadow;
- close fragment rather than a second conventional portrait;
- pause or separation with negative space;
- closing frame after the action.

Direct verbs, not poses. Give the subject something to notice, carry, open, pour, read, arrange, or move through. Preserve real facial texture and body proportions.

## 5. Food, table, and still life

Build 5–7 frames:

- table in its room or landscape;
- top or oblique view with asymmetrical breathing room;
- ingredient or material detail;
- hand entering to pour, cut, peel, or serve;
- one imperfect trace of use;
- light or shadow study;
- aftermath frame.

Use seasonal, local objects. Keep tableware tactile and plausible. Avoid overfilled tables, decorative clutter, perfect radial arrangements, and uniformly warm white balance.

## 6. Single-image generation prompt

Compose the prompt in this order:

1. original subject and quiet action;
2. specific place and spatial structure;
3. camera position and composition;
4. natural light and time;
5. palette and tactile surfaces;
6. one transient or lived-in detail;
7. optical behavior and finish;
8. exclusions.

Example:

> An original observational travel photograph of a woman opening pale wooden shutters in a modest island room, viewed from the shaded corridor through two layered doorways; she occupies the lower-right third while sunlit limewashed walls and negative space dominate the frame. Early side light, mineral blue and chalk palette, weathered stone floor, a linen curtain lifting in the sea breeze, shallow but plausible depth of field, gentle highlight roll-off, dimensional shadows, fine natural grain. Unstaged gesture, no tourism-ad gloss, no orange cast, no HDR, no plastic skin, no distorted architecture, no text or logos.

Adapt the example; do not reuse it mechanically.
